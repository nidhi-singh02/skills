# Voice profile: <your name / handle>

Save a filled-in copy as `~/.claude/voice-profile.md` (outside this repo). The skill reads it when
writing titles, descriptions and captions so the copy sounds like you. No profile? The skill asks
for 2-3 of your real posts and infers the voice from them.

## Style card (2-4 sentences)
Brief a ghostwriter: casing, energy (dry / warm / hype), and what makes it unmistakably you.

## Tone
- Qualities you want, and what you are NOT.

## Mechanics
- Sentence length and shape, punctuation tics, how you format lists and questions.

## Words and emoji
- Phrases you use; your emoji palette; words you would never use.

## Title formulas (YouTube, ≤60 chars)
- 2-3 patterns that are yours, e.g. `I made <thing> with <tool> in <time>`.

## Description structure
1. Line 1: what it is + tool (no overlap with the title).
2. 2-3 sentences of first-person context.
3. Your CTA line. 4. Links / contact line. 5. A comment-driving question, then hashtags.

## Instagram caption structure
Hook line -> what/how in 2 sentences -> CTAs (comment, save, send) -> hashtags.

## Always
- The CTA keyword matches the spoken word and the on-screen card.
- Confirm product/model names with the creator before posting.
